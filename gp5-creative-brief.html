Step 1: Review the Campaign Plan (Brian)

Your creative brief should be based on the campaign objective, target audience, and message strategy from Assignment 4.

Campaign Objective / Strategy Summary:

To address the business problem of low awareness and adoption of its high-margin custom gift box feature, CPP Farm Store is launching an integrated omnichannel campaign to drive product discovery and online adoption among students, families, and campus organizations. With a primary objective of increasing custom gift box orders by 40% during the spring peak gifting season from February to May, the campaign will utilize the central message “Thoughtful Gifts, Grown Here” to emphasize fresh, locally sourced, and highly customizable campus gifts. To effectively reach the target audience of campus affiliates who value meaningful gifts and convenient ordering, the strategy leverages a $5,000 total budget strategically weighted toward experiential campus events and activations (30%), targeted email marketing (25%), and high-intent Google Ads (18%). These coordinated paid and organic tactics will funnel traffic from discovery channels like Instagram, TikTok, and campus signage directly to the Shopify website, where customers can seamlessly configure their personalized boxes and finalize transactions with a frictionless in-store pickup option. Ultimately, campaign success will be rigorously measured against specific key performance indicators by targeting 5,000 unique website visits, a 3.0% e-commerce conversion rate, and $15,000 in total generated revenue, all while being supported by a continuous post-launch optimization strategy that mandates weekly performance reviews and bi-weekly A/B creative testing to maximize return on ad spend.

Step 4: Define the Key Message (Ron)

This six-word message drives the whole campaign. Thoughtful connects with anyone buying a gift that actually means something — whether that’s a student grabbing something for a friend’s graduation, a parent wanting to celebrate a milestone, or an alum who still feels tied to CPP. Gifts makes it clear right away that the Farm Store isn’t just a place to pick up produce — it’s a destination for something special. And Grown Here hits on two levels: it calls out the locally sourced, campus-grown quality of the products, and it taps into that sense of CPP pride that keeps people coming back.

What makes this message work is that it fits everyone we’re trying to reach without feeling generic. Students see it and feel the campus connection. Parents and alumni read it and trust that it’s something real and personal, not just another gift basket from a big box store. Local community members get the “local” signal without needing any explanation. It’s short enough to live on an Instagram caption, punchy enough to stand alone on a sign, and genuine enough to open an email — which is exactly what a message needs to do when it has to show up across this many channels.

Our Key Message: [“Thoughtful Gifts, Grown Here.”]

Step 5: Define Tone and Style (Alexa)

Our Tone and Style: For the CPP Farm Store campaign, the tone and style are designed to resonate directly with students, campus visitors, and the local community by blending approachability with a strong sense of local pride.

Friendly & Welcoming: Uses an inviting, warm voice that makes the store feel like an extension of the campus community rather than just a retail stop.

Fresh & Local: Highlights the authenticity of campus-grown produce and student-made goods, emphasizing quality and sustainability.

Student-Friendly & Practical: Keeps messaging straightforward, budget-conscious, and convenient for busy college schedules.

Visual Aesthetic: Clean, vibrant imagery featuring natural lighting, earthy tones (sage green, warm terracotta, and crisp white), and close-ups of fresh harvests to emphasize the farm-to-table appeal.

Original AI Output (Summary)

ChatGPT generated multiple marketing ideas, including Instagram captions, email subject lines, promotional headlines, calls to action, hashtags, and image concepts. The content emphasized customizable gift boxes, locally sourced products, seasonal occasions, and online ordering while providing several creative directions for the campaign.

Edits & Justification

What We Changed

  • Shortened the promotional copy to better fit each marketing channel.
  • Used the campaign slogan “Thoughtful Gifts, Grown Here.” consistently.
  • Added references to CPP Farm Store and Cal Poly Pomona.
  • Removed unsupported claims about discounts, delivery, pricing, and product availability.
  • Adjusted the messaging to better connect with students, parents, alumni, and local community members.
  • Simplified the visual concepts to make them realistic and appropriate for CPP Farm Store.

Why We Changed It

The AI-generated content provided useful ideas but required editing to accurately represent CPP Farm Store’s products, services, and campaign goals. Our group refined the messaging to ensure consistency with the campaign strategy, improve readability, and avoid misleading promotional claims.

Brand-Fit Explanation

The final creative assets reflect the CPP Farm Store brand by emphasizing fresh, locally sourced products, community support, and thoughtful gift giving. The messaging is friendly, welcoming, and student-focused while encouraging online ordering and increasing awareness of customizable gift boxes. The final assets align with the campaign objective of promoting seasonal gift boxes and strengthening the Farm Store’s connection with the Cal Poly Pomona community.
